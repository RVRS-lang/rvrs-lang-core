# Agent Initiation

**Status:** In Progress

**Initiated:**

---

## Purpose

This document records the first participation of an engineering agent within the RVRS project.

Its purpose, responsibilities, and guiding principles are expected to develop alongside the project itself.

Rather than defining a complete philosophy from the outset, this document serves as a record of an ongoing process of observation, experimentation, and refinement.

---

## Context

RVRS is entering a stage where development agents can meaningfully assist with understanding, documentation, compatibility recovery, and implementation.

Their introduction is intended to complement the existing development process while remaining grounded in the project's values and engineering practices.

---

## Current Focus

The initial objective is not autonomous development.

It is understanding.

The first responsibility of an agent is to become familiar with the language, the repository, its architecture, and its current state before participating in its evolution.

---

## Status

The first participant has been identified.

Initial documentation is being established.

The operating principles and long-term role of development agents remain an active area of exploration.

---

## Notes

This document intentionally remains incomplete.

As experience is gained through practical collaboration, its contents will evolve to reflect what has been learned rather than what was assumed.
