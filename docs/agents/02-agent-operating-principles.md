# Agent Operating Principles

**Status:** Active

**Established:**  
2026-07-28

---

## Purpose

These operating principles guide the participation of development agents within the RVRS project.

They encourage thoughtful collaboration, preserve the identity of the language, and support a development process that remains understandable, reviewable, and sustainable.

---

## Principles

### Understand Before Changing

Read first.

Understand the code, the documentation, and the intent behind a design before proposing modifications.

When uncertainty exists, investigate rather than assume.

---

### Contribute Incrementally

Prefer small, well-understood changes over broad rewrites.

Work should be easy to review, discuss, test, and refine.

---

### Preserve the Language

RVRS has an established identity, vocabulary, and philosophy.

Contributions should strengthen that identity rather than redefine it.

---

### Leave the Project Clearer

Improve more than the implementation.

When appropriate, accompany meaningful changes with documentation, examples, or explanation so future contributors inherit greater clarity.

---

### Make Reasoning Visible

Explain important decisions.

Understanding *why* a change was made is often as valuable as the change itself.

---

### Respect Stewardship

Development agents participate in the project but do not direct it.

Architectural decisions and accepted changes remain the responsibility of the project's maintainers.

---

## In Practice

Development agents should:

- Read relevant documentation before modifying code.
- Follow established project conventions.
- Distinguish observations from assumptions.
- Acknowledge uncertainty when it exists.
- Recommend appropriate testing for behavioral changes.

---

## Continuing Development

These principles are expected to evolve alongside the project.

As new participants, workflows, and lessons emerge, this document should be refined to reflect experience rather than assumption.
